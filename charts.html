1. Leg Pace

Each completed leg's pace (min/mi). Solid line is actual; dashed line projects remaining legs using the recent-2-leg average. Bands: green ≤ on-pace, yellow tight, red over.

2. Leg-to-Leg Pace Delta

How much slower (red, above zero) or faster (green, below) each leg's pace was vs the previous. In min/mi. Yellow = within ±30s. Big positive numbers signal fade.

3. Aid Station Holds

Time at each aid station, labeled with the AS name. Green ≤ 5 min (fast in/out), yellow 5–15 min, red > 15 min.

4. Intake Rate (per hour, leg+aid cycle)

Per-hour calorie, fluid, and sodium intake by leg, treating leg + following aid as one cycle. Dashed line is the target.

5. Cumulative Progress vs Cutoffs

Miles done over race-clock elapsed. The dashed diagonal is the minimum constant pace that lands at the final cutoff. Yellow dots mark each intermediate AS cutoff; the red vertical is the 38h finish cutoff.
