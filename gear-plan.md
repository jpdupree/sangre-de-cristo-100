# Power & Electronics Plan — Sangre de Cristo 100 (2026)

Context from 2025: no bank carried, one partial charge at mile 50, phone died ~7 AM
day two (~27h in) with overnight music — morale soundtrack lost exactly when the
hard hours started. This plan gives ~3× that margin.

## Hardware
- **2× Nitecore NB10000** (~150 g each) + **2× stubby USB-C cables** (one per bank)
- Phone: Galaxy Z Flip 6 (~4,000 mAh; IP48 — fine in rain, protect from dust)
- Watch: COROS APEX 2 (45h GPS rating; no working backup watch — PACE 2 has button issues)
- Headphones: Shokz — **one pair carried, spare pairs staged at Music Meadows and Horn Creek**
- Coros charging cable + Shokz charging cable staged at Music Meadows (backstop only)
- Junk-drawer bank in crew car as never-used deep reserve

## Protocol
1. **Bank rotation:** carry one NB10000; the other charges with crew at Music Meadows.
   Swap every pass — trade depleted for full, keep moving.
2. **Airplane mode on course** (GPS still works). Off at aid stations to text crew, back on when leaving.
3. **Cover screen only** for time/texts/music control — don't unfold on trail; keeps
   battery and keeps dust off the main screen. Zip-lock or closed pocket.
4. **Watch rule:** APEX 2 below 50% at any Music Meadows pass → charges off the power
   bank for ~20 min while hiking out.
5. **Phone = backup recorder:** if the watch dies, start recording in the Coros app
   (works in airplane mode). Verify phone-only recording once during a Build 3 long run.
6. **Cold:** phone + bank in chest pocket overnight; charge during warm daytime stretches.
7. **Music discipline:** no music until struggling — save the tool for when it matters.
   Pre-download generous offline library before race week: night-two playlist + podcast/audiobook.
8. 25W charging: a ~30-min aid stop restores ~50% phone if ever needed beyond swaps.

## Rehearsals

- Build 3 long run (Aug 8): verify phone-only Coros recording; practice cover-screen-only use.
